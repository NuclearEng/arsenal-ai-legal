Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

MoundOS (“we,” “our,” or “us”) explains how we collect, use, store, and delete information when you use the MoundOS iOS app (including optional Apple Watch features). This policy applies to the shipping product surface described below.

Key points
  • Pitch video analysis runs on your device
  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not track you across apps or websites for advertising (no ATT / IDFA advertising use)
  • You own your data. You can export or delete it in the app
  • Optional iCloud sync uses your own private iCloud database; we cannot read it
  • Your athlete model is trained on your device
  • MoundOS does not use the microphone
  • Community / social messaging is not enabled in this release

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you create in the app

  • Session and pitch data: estimated velocity and break, pitch type labels, notes, timestamps, and spin only when you import a radar file (labeled “From your radar”)
  • Videos and clips: recordings you capture (stored in the app on your iPhone, in Documents/PitchClips, encrypted by iOS until you first unlock the phone after it starts)
  • Settings: preferences such as capture mode, mound distance, handedness, display options
  • Performance assessments / recovery logs: values you enter for training or sleep-related features
  • Lift log: exercises, sets, reps, load, effort (RPE), and optional body weight you enter
  • Athlete profile and model: optional age, height, and weight you type in (or, for height, fill in from Apple Health; see 1.3), and the per-athlete model the app computes on your device from your sessions and lifts
  • Body composition (DEXA), optional: if you import a scan report (PDF or photo), the app reads it on your device and keeps only the values you confirm (lean/fat mass, body-fat %, bone density, regional lean mass). The report file itself is not stored or uploaded
  • Recruiting card, optional: the name, school, contact details, and athlete photo you add. The photo is added only with a parent or guardian’s agreement when that is required

1.2 Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, MoundOS stores these in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only (they are not synced through iCloud Keychain):

  • Apple user identifier
  • Display name (if Apple provides it)
  • Email address (if Apple provides it)

Guest use is available without creating an account. Core pitch capture and analysis do not require Sign in with Apple.

1.3 Apple Health (optional)

Apple Health is used only if you turn on Readiness. For a child under 13, or when no age range was shared, a parent or guardian turns it on. You can say no to any type and still use the app.

  • iPhone reads: sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen, and active energy. Readiness compares these with your own last 28 days and can only suggest a lighter throwing day
  • iPhone reads, height: only when you tap Fill in from Apple Health while setting up your profile. MoundOS asks for height on its own at that moment, reads your most recent height once, and shows it for you to confirm before saving it to your profile. It is not read again in the background
  • iPhone writes: nothing. MoundOS on iPhone does not save anything to Apple Health
  • Apple Watch reads: heart rate while a pitching session is open, heart rate variability and resting heart rate when you open the recovery page, and sleep when you open the sleep page. The Watch does not read active energy. It asks for access when you start a session or open those pages, not when the app opens. A pitching session is not saved to Apple Health
  • A pitching session is not saved as an Apple Health workout. Apple Health is read for readiness (sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability).

Readiness values read from Apple Health are kept in memory; they are not saved by MoundOS. While Readiness is on, Apple Health can wake MoundOS in the background when new sleep, resting heart rate, or heart rate variability arrives, so today’s readiness stays current without opening the app. Today’s readiness level and score are sent to MoundOS on your paired Apple Watch, and appear in a notification only if you turn on the morning readiness reminder. A session’s heart rate summary (from your Apple Watch or a Bluetooth sensor) is saved with that session on your iPhone. Health data is removed before iCloud sync, team sharing, and default exports. We do not use it for advertising, data mining, or sale, and we do not send it to a server.

1.4 Bluetooth heart rate sensors (optional)

If you tap Find sensors, MoundOS asks for Bluetooth so it can pair a standard heart rate sensor, such as a chest strap, an arm band, or an Amazfit watch with heart rate push turned on. It reads only the standard heart rate measurement during a pitching session, and only while Readiness is on. The readings stay on your iPhone with that session.

1.5 Location (optional, outdoor air only)

MoundOS asks for your location only while you use the app and only when you ask for outdoor air, so temperature, humidity, and wind can be included in the pitch physics. You can skip it; the app then uses the phone’s pressure sensor and standard air.

  • MoundOS asks for your approximate location, not your precise location, unless you choose otherwise in the prompt
  • Before any weather request, the coordinates are rounded to 2 decimal places (about 1 kilometer). Your precise location never leaves the phone
  • The rounded coordinates go to Apple WeatherKit. If WeatherKit does not answer, MoundOS uses standard air and does not send the location anywhere else
  • Location is not stored, not used for advertising, and not needed for core pitch analysis
  • MoundOS on Apple Watch does not use location

1.6 Camera, microphone, photos, motion

  • Camera: required for live pitch capture and analysis
  • Microphone: not used. Clips are recorded without sound, and MoundOS never asks for microphone access
  • Saving to Photos: finished pitch clips are saved to your Photos library by default, with add-only access (MoundOS cannot see the rest of your library). You can turn this off in Settings
  • Choosing photos or videos: only through the system photo picker, which shares just the item you pick
  • Motion and pressure sensors: used to check that the phone is steady and to estimate the air for pitch physics. On Apple Watch, during a pitching session you start, the motion sensors measure the arm wearing the Watch: each throw’s arm speed, wrist acceleration, timing, arm slot, and a relative workload estimate. Only those per-throw numbers are sent to MoundOS on your iPhone and saved with that session; the raw sensor readings are not kept. Like health data, arm motion is removed before iCloud sync, team sharing, and default exports, and is not used for advertising or sent to a server

  • On-device logs and crash information you may share when contacting support

1.8 What we do not collect for advertising

  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
  • Third-party ad network profiles
  • Contacts harvesting for marketing databases

1.9 iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your sessions, lift log, templates, profile, and model state are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account (container iCloud.com.cis-consulting.MoundOS). Apple hosts this data under its terms; we have no access to it. Pitch video clips, Health data, and body-composition values are not synced. Sync is off when you’re signed out of iCloud. You can turn it off in Settings. Deleting your account in the app deletes the synced data; you can also remove it in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → MoundOS.

1.10 Team (staged, TestFlight only)

Team is being tested and is available only in TestFlight builds; it is off in the App Store release. When it is on, a coach creates a team and shares an invite code. An athlete who joins shares only a small summary with that coach: the display name they choose (initials or a nickname are fine), graduation year, recent velocity estimates, strike percentage, and pitch and session counts. Video, contact details, Health data, and location are never shared. Athletes under 18 need a parent or guardian’s consent before joining. The invite and join records are kept in the app’s iCloud public database, readable only with the invite code; the summary is shared from the athlete’s private iCloud to the coach only. Either side can stop sharing at any time, and deleting your account leaves your team (or deletes a team you run).

1.11 Learning from your sessions (opt-in)

A setting named “Help make MoundOS smarter” is off until you turn it on. Sharing is not turned on in this version, so nothing leaves this iPhone. If a later version turns sharing on, and only while the setting is on, MoundOS may share pitches you pair with a radar reading: the camera estimates, the radar number, the pitch type if you confirmed or corrected it, the camera and air settings, and the day. It never shares video, photos, your name, your team, your school, or your location. It would send only on Wi-Fi. You can turn the setting off and ask us to delete what you shared.

Athletes under 13, and anyone who has not shared an age range, are not included until a parent or guardian agrees on this phone. Ages 13 to 17 can opt in, and a parent or guardian can see that setting. You can turn it off at any time. “Delete my contributed data” queues deletion of contributed samples and clears the choice on this phone.

Tuning readings to this phone stays on the device, works without that opt-in, and is undone if it is worse than the original model. Uploading samples and shared-model updates stay off until a host exists and this policy and the App Privacy label are updated.

1.12 Staff capture

A hidden tool for MoundOS staff can record full video and upload it only after a signed release, and only with a parent or guardian release when the participant is under 18. It is not available to customers. The version line in About does nothing unless the Apple account is on a staff list.

2. Community / social features

This release does not enable Community messaging, partner discovery, or public social profiles. If social features return in a future version, we will update this policy to describe profiles, messages, CloudKit-backed content, filtering, reporting, and retention before those surfaces ship.

3. How we use information

  • Provide pitch analysis estimates and session history
  • Power optional Readiness and heart rate features you turn on
  • Train your personal athlete model and produce training recommendations, on your device
  • Sync your data to your private iCloud when you turn it on
  • Process in-app purchases via Apple StoreKit
  • Respond to support requests you send
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs

4. Storage, security, and retention

On-device processing: Pitch computer-vision analysis runs on your device.

Local storage: Sessions, clips, and preferences live in the app on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption, and may be included in your personal iCloud device backup if you turn on device backup.

Retention: We retain data on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or use in-app delete. Support emails you send may be retained as needed to respond.

Third parties: Apple provides StoreKit, HealthKit, WeatherKit, Sign in with Apple, and (when used) CloudKit under Apple’s terms. Cloudflare runs the service that disconnects Sign in with Apple when you delete your account; it receives only Apple’s one-time code and stores nothing. We require that service providers who process personal data for us provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access / export: Export sessions where the app provides export tools (free exports carry a MoundOS watermark; Player exports do not)
  • Delete all local data: Settings → Privacy → Delete All Data (includes lift log, athlete model, profile, photo, and body-composition values on this iPhone)
  • Delete account: Account → Delete Account. This deletes your private iCloud copy, takes you off any team (or deletes a team you run), and removes your local data and the Sign in with Apple details stored on this iPhone. If you used Sign in with Apple, MoundOS also asks Apple to revoke the app’s access, so MoundOS no longer appears under Sign in with Apple in your Apple ID settings. To do this, the app sends a one-time code from Apple to a small MoundOS service run on Cloudflare, which passes it to Apple and keeps nothing. If that fails, the app shows the steps to do it yourself (iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → MoundOS → Stop Using Apple ID). Deleting does not cancel App Store subscriptions—manage those in Apple ID subscription settings
  • Delete synced iCloud data yourself: iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → MoundOS
  • Permissions: Turn off camera, Photos, Health, Bluetooth, location, and motion access in iOS Settings → MoundOS, and Health access in the Health app

6. Children

MoundOS is a sports training tool used by many young athletes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for commercial profiles. For a child under 13, or when no age range was shared, a parent or guardian must agree before Readiness, the athlete photo, or learning from sessions can be turned on, and athletes under 18 need a parent or guardian’s consent to join a team. Parents/guardians should supervise use. The app is not directed to the Kids Category with social features in this release.

7. International users

You may contact us to exercise access, deletion, or other rights available under local law.
