Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Arsenal AI (“we,” “our,” or “us”) explains how we collect, use, store, and delete information when you use the Arsenal AI iOS app (including optional Apple Watch features). This policy applies to the shipping product surface described below.

Key points
  • Pitch video analysis runs on your device
  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not track you across apps or websites for advertising (no ATT / IDFA advertising use)
  • You can export or delete your local data in the app
  • Community / social messaging is not enabled in this release

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you create in the app

  • Session and pitch data: estimated velocity, spin, break, pitch type labels, notes, timestamps
  • Videos and clips: recordings you capture or save (stored in the app sandbox, e.g. Documents/PitchClips)
  • Settings: preferences such as capture mode, mound distance, handedness, display options
  • Performance assessments / recovery logs: values you enter for training or sleep-related features

1.2 Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we may store on device:

  • Apple user identifier
  • Display name (if provided)
  • Email address (if provided)

Guest use is available without creating an account. Core pitch capture and analysis do not require Sign in with Apple.

1.3 HealthKit (optional)

If you grant Health access, we may read and/or write the following types as used by Recovery, sleep coaching, and workout features:

  • Read (examples used by the app): workouts, active energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), oxygen saturation (SpO₂), respiratory rate, sleep analysis, step count
  • Write: workout samples (duration and estimated energy when you save a pitching/training workout)

Health data stays under Apple Health’s protections. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, data mining, or sale. Energy written to Health may be estimated from duration and should not be treated as measured calorimetry.

1.4 Location (limited / optional)

  • Apple Watch / environmental features: when-in-use location may be requested for facility or environmental context where that feature is enabled
  • App Clip: location confirmation may be requested by the system for App Clip invocation when an App Clip is offered; this release does not rely on social geo-discovery

We do not use location for advertising. We do not require location for core pitch analysis on iPhone.

1.5 Camera, microphone, photos, motion

  • Camera: required for live pitch capture and analysis
  • Microphone: may be used when recording video with audio
  • Photos: only when you pick media via the system photo picker
  • Motion / sensors: may support capture stabilization or Watch sensors

1.6 Device and diagnostics

  • Device model and OS version for compatibility
  • On-device logs and crash information you may share when contacting support

1.7 What we do not collect for advertising

  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking
  • Third-party ad network profiles
  • Contacts harvesting for marketing databases

2. Community / social features

This release does not enable Community messaging, partner discovery, or public social profiles. If social features return in a future version, we will update this policy to describe profiles, messages, CloudKit-backed content, filtering, reporting, and retention before those surfaces ship.

3. How we use information

  • Provide pitch analysis estimates and session history
  • Power optional Recovery / HealthKit coaching features you enable
  • Process in-app purchases via Apple StoreKit
  • Respond to support requests you send
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs

4. Storage, security, and retention

On-device processing: Pitch computer-vision analysis runs on your device.

Local storage: Sessions, clips, and preferences live in the app sandbox (and may be included in your personal iCloud device backup if you enable device backup).

Retention: We retain data on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or use in-app delete. Support emails you send may be retained as needed to respond.

Third parties: Apple provides StoreKit, HealthKit, Sign in with Apple, and (when used) CloudKit under Apple’s terms. We require that service providers who process personal data for us provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access / export: Export sessions where the app provides export tools (export of reports may require Pro)
  • Delete all local data: Settings → Privacy → Delete All Data
  • Delete account: Account → Delete Account (removes Sign in with Apple identity stored by the app and local data). This does not cancel App Store subscriptions—manage those in Apple ID subscription settings
  • Permissions: Revoke camera, Health, location, etc. in iOS Settings → Arsenal AI

6. Children

Arsenal AI is a sports training tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for commercial profiles. Parents/guardians should supervise use. The app is not directed to the Kids Category with social features in this release.

7. International users

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we process data to provide the service you request (contract), with legitimate interests in security and product reliability, and with consent for optional Health or location features. You may contact us to exercise access, deletion, or other rights available under local law.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date and the in-app document.
