Your iPhone is now a pitching tracker.

Set the phone to the side of the mound, record a bullpen, and MoundOS shows speed and movement estimates, suggests the pitch type, and keeps every session so you can see yourself get better.

Download on the App Store

Speed and movement

Camera estimates with an honest give-or-take range, so you know how far to trust each number.

Pitch type

An on-device suggestion labeled Beta, with a confidence level and a one-tap correction.

Best Plan

Where to aim each pitch by count and batter side, and what to throw next, from your own pitches.

Progress you can see

Weekly goals, streaks, a Sunday recap, workload and rest guidance, and widgets on your Home Screen.

Share the good ones

Turn a pitch into a vertical clip with labeled speed and movement. Children need a parent or guardian to agree first.

Private by design

Video stays on your iPhone unless you share it. No account needed. Optional private iCloud sync.

MoundOS measures with the camera, not radar. Spin is shown only when you import it from your own radar device. Pitch type is a Beta and can be wrong.
